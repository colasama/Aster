# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent,
criticize ideas rather than people, and make technical decisions with evidence. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, or sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact channel. Reports will be
handled promptly, confidentially, and without retaliation.
